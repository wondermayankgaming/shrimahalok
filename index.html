About Us

A Dhaba Table for Every Occasion

Shri Mahalok Restaurant & Dhaba sits on Tarana Road in Barkhedi Jagir, Ujjain, serving pure vegetarian food the way it's meant to be — generous, unhurried and full of flavor. Our kitchen moves easily between comfort and celebration: a quick plate of chaat and chaas for travellers passing through, and full spreads of paneer, kofta and thali for families settling in.

Alongside the everyday menu, we take special orders for birthdays, kitty parties and wedding functions — so whatever you're celebrating, there's a table and a menu ready for it.
